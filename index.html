Core Theoretical Insight

K-Means optimizes within-cluster variance assuming isotropic convex clusters, causing it to fail on non-linear manifolds like Two Moons or Concentric Circles. MST Single-Linkage successfully traverses arbitrary manifold topologies, but basic longest-edge cutting is sensitive to noise bridges between clusters.
